Notice. The package contains no texts of the documents collected by the InfoStream system and no verbatim quotes from them: texts are replaced by SHA-256 and quotes by references to their place in a paragraph. Short text fragments remain in the records and verdicts (entity names, titles of documents that the models extracted as entities, phrases in the qualifiers and in the reasons of the auditors; fragments of 40 characters or more together cover 7.4% of the corpus text), and the manifests and inputs contain the document URLs. The rights to the texts of the documents belong to their publishers; the CC BY 4.0 license does not extend to them.
